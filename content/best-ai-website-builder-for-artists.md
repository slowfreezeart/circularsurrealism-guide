Search "best AI website builder for artists" and you get ranked lists, mostly written by companies that sell one of the builders on them. Very few are by an artist who actually finished a big site with one.

This one is by an artist who did. [circularsurrealism.com](https://circularsurrealism.com) is an AI Art site with 440+ works in 27 collections, vibe coded in Grok Build by Slowfreeze, an AI artist who is not a developer. It took about 8 weeks and 400+ prompts, from 27 August to 23 October 2026.

To be clear about what this article is and isn't: **Slowfreeze built only with Grok Build.** The site was not built with Lovable, Bolt, Wix, Squarespace or Framer, so there are no scores, ratings or "we tested" claims for them here. Everything said about those tools comes from their own official pages, checked on 26 October 2026 and linked. Plans change, so open the links before you decide.

## What does an artist actually need from a website builder?

A real build ran into questions the template lists skip:

- **Can it hold your whole body of work?** Hundreds of pieces, each with its own page, collection, year, edition and links to where it can be bought.
- **Does it survive video and large files?** On the case-study site, a mosaic of every work crashed phones when each tile loaded the original file. It held once the tiles became small images. A publish with about 86 videos (about 120 MB) froze on "Deploying" and went through once it was cut to 25 files (about 34 MB).
- **Can it show what is for sale, by itself?** Here, live listings are checked about every 90 seconds from four marketplaces (Transient Labs, Manifold, Objkt and Networked.art), so nobody has to type "sold" by hand.
- **Can you use your own domain, and do you own a copy of the files?**
- **Does it make the work look as good or better?** A 3D gallery was tried and dropped the same day because the images "did not load with the HDR they need in order to shine and glow."

If your answer is "a dozen pieces, no video, an enquiry button", most builders here will do. The bigger the site, the more the type of builder matters.

## What's the difference between an AI website builder and a vibe-coding app builder?

The tools fall into two families.

**Template builders with AI on top: Wix, Squarespace and Framer.** You start from a prompt or a template, then edit visually. Wix describes going "from a prompt to a business-ready website" and then flowing between its AI agent and drag-and-drop editing ([Wix AI website builder](https://www.wix.com/ai-website-builder)). Squarespace lists "Built-in AI Tools" and a drag-and-drop editor on every plan ([Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)). Framer sells a visual site builder with AI agents and a CMS ([Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing)).

**Chat builders that write the code: Grok Build, Lovable and Bolt.** You describe the site in a conversation, the builder writes the code, and you check a live preview. Lovable says you "build by chatting" and, "if you do code, you can view and edit everything Lovable writes" ([Lovable portfolio builder](https://lovable.dev/build/portfolio-website-builder)). No template ceiling, but every change goes through the chat, and fixes cost credits or tokens.

## What was building an AI Art site in Grok Build really like?

One site, one conversation. Slowfreeze described a room, looked at the live preview, and said what was wrong until it was right. When written words failed, a screenshot with arrows worked. Two captions sat on the wrong photos, and a red double arrow between them fixed it on the next try.

![A real screenshot marked with arrows to show the builder exactly what to switch](visuals/f3-screenshot-arrows.png)

**What it made possible:** an archive of every work, one page per collection and per piece, a sales ledger, live "Listed" and "Sold" marks, a print shop, a gallery that hangs works in their real shape, a zoom view, a mosaic of the whole body of work, and themes. None of it came from a template. The builder counted about 45,000 lines in the final site.

**What went wrong, honestly:**

- **The preview was lost** on 2 to 3 September. The rebuild was missing features, and about $5 of credits went into it before the approach changed. What worked was restoring from the live site. The full story is in [the lost-preview recovery guide]({{HUB_URL}}/grok-build-lost-preview-recovery/).
- **Publishing stalled twice:** once on that 120 MB of video, and once for about a day, until the site's framework got a security update.
- **The custom domain** was set up from the desktop website, not the phone app, and `www` needed a redirect at the registrar.
- **The mosaic crashed phones** until its tiles were made light. See [8 fixes for slow AI-built image and video sites]({{HUB_URL}}/make-ai-built-website-load-faster/).

![86 videos (about 120 MB) stuck on Deploying, versus 25 files (about 34 MB) that published](visuals/s4-publish-weight.png)

**What it cost:** the build ran on the $11-a-month X plan at the time, plus at least $75 in $5 credit packs. Prices and plans change, so check the current ones. Several features needed as many fix rounds as first attempts, so budget for fixes, not just features.

**What you own:** the domain, the live site and the files. The files were exported to GitHub. In Slowfreeze's words, "This is the part I wish I had done earlier." The steps are in [Grok Build: publish, custom domain and GitHub backup]({{HUB_URL}}/grok-build-publish-custom-domain-github/).

## How do Lovable and Bolt compare? (from their official pages)

Slowfreeze didn't build with either. This is what their own pages say as of 26 October 2026.

**Lovable**
- It's free to start. The free plan includes a daily grant of 5 build credits, up to 30 a month ([Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing)).
- Hosting is included, on a Lovable address. Connecting your own domain needs a paid plan ([Lovable portfolio builder](https://lovable.dev/build/portfolio-website-builder)).
- It offers two-way GitHub sync, available on all plans, and repositories are private by default ([Lovable GitHub docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/github)).
- For media-heavy artists: Lovable "cannot save files larger than 10 MB into your project", and for large media its docs suggest asking it to "Migrate large files to CDN assets" ([Lovable GitHub docs](https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/github)).

**Bolt**
- There's a free plan with 1M tokens a month and Bolt branding on websites ([Bolt pricing](https://bolt.new/pricing)).
- Custom domains and removing the branding come with Pro. File uploads are limited to 10 MB on Free and 100 MB on Pro ([Bolt pricing](https://bolt.new/pricing)).
- It connects to GitHub, commits your changes automatically, and lets you publish through Bolt hosting, Netlify or another service ([Bolt GitHub docs](https://support.bolt.new/integrations/git)).

Same family as Grok Build, so the lessons above apply: catalog first, light media, back up early.

## How do Wix, Squarespace and Framer compare? (from their official pages)

Again, Slowfreeze did not build with these. From their pages, as of 26 October 2026:

**Wix**
- Creating a site is free. You need a Premium plan to connect a custom domain or collect payments ([Wix AI website builder](https://www.wix.com/ai-website-builder)).
- It builds a site from a prompt, then you edit with the AI agent or by drag and drop ([Wix plans](https://www.wix.com/plans)).

**Squarespace**
- There's no free plan. Every site starts with a 14-day free trial ([Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)).
- It has built-in ecommerce. The Basic plan charges a 2% online store transaction fee, and Core and above charge 0% ([Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)).
- Video hosting is limited by plan, with 30 minutes on Basic ([Squarespace pricing](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing)).

**Framer**
- There's a free plan on a Framer domain. Connecting a custom domain needs a paid plan ([Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing)).
- It has a CMS for collections of content, and its AI agents run on credits ([Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing)).

## So which AI website builder is best for artists?

It depends on the site you need, not on a ranking.

- **You want a clean portfolio and a print shop live this weekend.** A template builder is probably the better fit. Squarespace and Wix have checkout, hosting and editing built in, and you won't spend credits on fix rounds.
- **You think visually and want to place every element yourself.** Framer's visual editor may suit you better than describing changes in a chat.
- **You want chat-built code with GitHub sync and you're comparing plans.** Lovable and Bolt document that clearly on their own pages.
- **Your site is an archive: hundreds of works, video, live sale marks from marketplaces, rooms no template has.** This is where a chat builder earned its keep in the case study, and it's what Grok Build delivered here, with the caveats above. It took weeks, not minutes. Whatever you choose, the method is the same: [start from your content]({{HUB_URL}}/how-to-build-a-website-with-ai/) and [give every room one job]({{HUB_URL}}/build-an-artist-portfolio-website-with-ai/).
